Detecting and tracking road users, computing corridor geometry, and rendering HUD.

How the decision works

  1. Detect & Track: Identifies road users (person, bicycle, motorcycle, car, bus, truck) and smooths boxes with EMA (α=0.45) + 0.4 s dropout coasting.
  2. Ego Corridor: Projects a relative trapezoid (bottom 0.30–0.70 width to horizon y=0.45) that works on both 16:9 landscape and 9:16 reels.
  3. Scale-Invariant Gap: Measures the widest free lateral gap in the nearest occupied depth band, normalised by median vehicle width at that same band.
  4. Calibrated Verdict: Fitted thresholds (SAFE ≥ 1.8, TIGHT ≥ 0.4) with center-line coverage & TTC overrides, stabilised by a 2.0 s median filter and 0.5 s hysteresis.

Verdicts describe free lateral space in the ego corridor, not overall traffic safety. Ranges are monocular estimates from assumed object heights and a 78° HFOV, not sensor readings.
