How a verdict is read

gap ratio = free space / median vehicle width
SAFE Free gap at least 1.8 vehicle-widths at the nearest occupied depth band.
TIGHT A gap between 0.4 and 1.8; committing is a squeeze.
BLOCKED Under 0.4, or the centre line is covered, or a blocker closes inside 1.5 s.
UNKNOWN Not enough evidence: the stabiliser abstains until its 2 s window fills.

How the corridor is scored

relative coordinates, no camera calibration
  1. 1

    Detect and track

    Six road-user classes, boxes smoothed with an EMA of 0.45 and coasted through 0.4 s of dropout.

  2. 2

    Measure the nearest band

    Users are bucketed at 3 m and 8 m from an assumed height; only the nearest occupied band is scored, and its free gap is divided by the median vehicle width there.

  3. 3

    Stabilise

    Fitted thresholds with centre-line and collision overrides, then a 2 s median and 0.5 s hysteresis on top.

A verdict describes free lateral space in the ego corridor, not whether the pass is safe; it is not rider assistance. Ranges come from assumed object heights and a 78° horizontal field of view, so treat metres as relative.
